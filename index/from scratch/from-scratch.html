Supply Chain: Key Process Areas

  1. Procurement
    • Supplier sourcing and evaluation
    • Purchase order management
    • Contract negotiation
  2. Inventory Management
    • Stock level monitoring
    • Demand forecasting
    • Warehouse organization
  3. Logistics & Distribution
    • Transportation routing
    • Last-mile delivery
    • Returns processing
  4. Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP)
    • Cross-functional team alignment
    • Capacity planning
    • Performance metrics review
Why this matters: Industrial Distribution sits at the intersection of all four of these areas — understanding the full chain is what separates great sales engineers from average ones.

About Me

Howdy! I am Aryana Rabee, Class of 2028 at Texas A&M University, currently studying Industrial Distribution.

I aspire to become a sales engineer, but before entering that career field, I believe it is essential to understand the logistics and operational processes that happen behind the scenes of a company.

Strong sales professionals are not only skilled communicators, but they also understand supply chains, inventory management, procurement, and the systems that keep businesses running efficiently.
